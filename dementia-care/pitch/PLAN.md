# pitch - plan (suggestions, create your own files)

Proving it works. Judges score innovation, functionality, user experience, scalability and real-world impact.

## Suggested sub-folders (tasks)
- script/       3-minute script: problem (with a SOURCED India statistic), live demo, differentiation, scalability and the ask.
                Demo order: game adapts -> walk out of safe zone -> SOS -> 30-day trend with flagged change.
- qa/           Judge questions with answers: difference from existing apps, privacy (consent, DPDP Act 2023 - check current rules),
                clinical validity ('we do not diagnose; change from own baseline'), scalability, what is stubbed.
- deck/         Short deck: problem, solution, architecture, differentiation, roadmap (ABDM readiness, clinician reports, NGO pilot).
- demo-data/    30 days of realistic sessions for one patient with a believable dip, plus 2 or 3 extra patients for the NGO view.
- testing/      Test checklist: full flow on two phones, airplane mode, Hindi voice, battery-saver phone, three clean runs in a row. Bug list.
- backup/       Screen recording of the full demo and an offline fallback plan. Charge devices.

## Talks to
Everyone. Needs working features by about hour 17; freeze at hour 19; rehearsal after.

## Done when
The demo runs clean three times in a row and a backup video exists.

## Watch out
Say honestly what is stubbed (video call, doctor connect, ABHA). Never claim cure, treatment or diagnosis.
