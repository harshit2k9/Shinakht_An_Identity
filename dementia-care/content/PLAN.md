# content - plan (suggestions, create your own files)

Everything the patient reads, hears and sees. Needs kindness and care, little coding.

## Suggested sub-folders (tasks)
- words/        Word-Picture Match: id, English, Hindi, image file, level. Start with about 30 familiar everyday objects.
- images/       License-safe images only. Keep SOURCES.md with where each image came from.
- stories/      Short stories (Hindi + English) with 2 or 3 simple questions each, in levels. About 5 to start.
- strings/      Screen texts in Hindi + English, reminder wording, assistant fallback messages.
- review/       A native Hindi speaker checks tone and spelling. Keep sentences short, warm, familiar, never childish.

## Talks to
patient/games (agree the file format first, JSON works), core/l10n (strings), functions/assistant (fallback text).

## Done when
At least 30 words and 5 stories exist, reviewed, and a game round runs with them.

## Watch out
Choose familiar items for North Indian elders. No medical claims in any text.
